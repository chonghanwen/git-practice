# Management Account Review Instructions

Review the monthly management accounts from a Finance Director perspective.

Focus on:

1. Revenue performance
2. Gross profit and GP margin
3. Operating expenses
4. Cash flow
5. Balance sheet movements
6. Significant month-to-month variances
7. Significant year-to-date variances
8. Unusual transactions
9. Potential accounting errors
10. Missing schedules or supporting documents

The review should focus on material issues rather than small accounting differences.

Provide a management-level summary highlighting:

- Key findings
- Risks
- Issues requiring investigation
- Recommended follow-up actions
