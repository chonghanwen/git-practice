# Review Rules

## Variance Review

Highlight significant variances where:

- Percentage variance is greater than 10%; or
- Absolute variance is greater than RM100,000.

## Revenue

Identify:

- Significant revenue decline
- Unusual revenue increases
- Revenue inconsistent with previous months

## Gross Profit

Review:

- GP margin movement
- Significant deterioration in margin
- Unusual improvement requiring explanation

## Operating Expenses

Identify:

- Large increases
- New expenses
- Unusual expenses
- Recurring expenses significantly above budget

## Balance Sheet

Review significant movements in:

- Cash
- Receivables
- Payables
- Borrowings
- Director/shareholder balances
- Other material accounts
